# Meal photos

Stock photos from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, no attribution required). Each file is named for its Pexels photo ID: `images/meals/<id>.jpg` is https://www.pexels.com/photo/<id>/.

They are **illustrative**: chosen to look like the dish type, not photos of these exact recipes. Add your own photo to any meal (meal page, "Add your photo"); your photo always replaces the stock one.
