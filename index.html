How to Start

Small steps, in this order.

1. Establish your meal routine first

Start with meals you already enjoy: cheesy chicken pita sandwiches, smoothies, chicken tacos, shrimp, and seasoned vegetables. You don't need to eat stereotypical breakfast foods or force yourself to like meals you dislike.

2. Start exercising in small increments

Begin with 10–15 minutes of comfortable movement when you're ready. Walking, beginner workouts, and gentle strength exercises are all options. You don't have to wait until your diet is perfect to start moving.

3. Increase gradually

Build consistency before increasing duration. Eventually, you may enjoy morning and evening sessions, but an hour twice a day isn't a requirement for successful weight loss.

4. Fuel your activity

Eat enough to support your energy, recovery, and nutritional needs. We shouldn't make exercise a way to compensate for eating or use a very low calorie limit as the foundation of your plan.

5. Keep taking your medications

Make sure you are also taking your ADHD medications, depression medications, and anxiety medications as prescribed. This plan works alongside them, not instead of them. Talk to your prescriber or doctor before changing anything.
